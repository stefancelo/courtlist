About CourtList

Why we built it

A Euroleague game night involves dozens of small jobs: referee crews to confirm, press passes to approve, LED boards and shot clocks to test, highlights to publish and travel to book for the next away game. Operations staff usually track all of this across spreadsheets, emails and chat threads.

CourtList puts every matchday task in one place. Staff add tasks with a due date and priority, move them across a Kanban board as work progresses, and see at a glance what is still open before tip-off.

Features

  • Tasks with due dates and colour-coded priorities
  • Live search, status and priority filters, sorting
  • Kanban board with drag and drop
  • Charts by status and priority
  • Latest activity feed and host city weather
  • Dark mode that remembers your choice
